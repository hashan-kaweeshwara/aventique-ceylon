Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common questions about traveling to Sri Lanka.

What is the best time to visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is a year-round destination! The west and south coasts and the hill country are best from December to March, while the east coast is ideal from April to September. This means there's always a sunny side of the island to explore. The shoulder months of April and October can offer great deals with pleasant weather on most of the island.

Do I need a visa to visit Sri Lanka?

Most visitors need an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETA) which can be obtained online at www.eta.gov.lk before departure. The ETA grants a 30-day tourist visa which can be extended up to 90 days at the Department of Immigration in Colombo. The process is straightforward and typically approved within 24 hours. We're happy to assist you with the application process.

What languages are spoken in Sri Lanka?

The official languages are Sinhala and Tamil. English is widely spoken in tourist areas, hotels, restaurants, and shops, so communication is rarely an issue for international visitors. All our guides are fluent in English and can communicate complex historical and cultural information with ease. We also have guides who speak French, German, and Mandarin available on request.

Is Sri Lanka safe for tourists?

Yes, Sri Lanka is widely considered one of the safest countries in Asia for tourists. The Sri Lankan people are renowned for their warmth and hospitality. Standard travel precautions apply — take care of your valuables, stay aware of your surroundings, and respect local customs. Our team provides 24/7 support throughout your trip to ensure your safety and comfort at all times.

What currency is used in Sri Lanka?

The official currency is the Sri Lankan Rupee (LKR). US Dollars and Euros are widely accepted at hotels and larger shops. ATMs are readily available in cities and tourist areas. We recommend carrying some local currency for smaller purchases, tipping, and rural areas. Currency exchange is available at the airport, banks, and authorized exchange centers throughout the island.
