Our Story

Founded in 2016, Aventique Ceylon was born from a deep love for Sri Lanka and a desire to share its hidden treasures with the world. What started as a small passion project by a group of local travel enthusiasts has grown into one of Sri Lanka's most trusted travel agencies.

With over a decade of combined experience in tourism, our team has explored every corner of this beautiful island. From the ancient ruins of Anuradhapura to the untouched beaches of the east coast, we've mapped out experiences that go far beyond the ordinary guidebook.

We believe that the best travel experiences come from genuine connections with local communities, sustainable practices, and a deep respect for the natural and cultural heritage of Sri Lanka. Every journey we craft is designed to leave our travelers with memories that last a lifetime and a deeper understanding of this remarkable island.
